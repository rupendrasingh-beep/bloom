Rayaan Story

Founded in
1984 by Khalid Bin Mohammed Al Rabban

Drawn from a natural reservoir, Rayyan delivers pure, high-quality mineral water with world-class standards and commitment to sustainability.

Mission and Vision

Pioneering natural refreshment through innovation, care, and sustainability.

Founder

Khalid Bin Mohammed Al Rabban

  1. 1984

    Rayyan launched at “aquifer-to-bottle” desert site

  2. 1996

    First in Qatar/Middle East to shift to PET preforms & in-house bottle making

  3. 2000

    Upgraded to automated tech; built in-house quality control labs

  4. 2011

    HACCP certified; reinforced ISO 9001 systems for food safety and quality

  5. 2018

  6. 2025

    Team scaled to 500–1,000+ across corporate & manufacturing
